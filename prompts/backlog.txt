You are given a software project brief, and the list of units another system
split it into. Each unit will be built separately, in the order given. Judge only
whether the split follows from the brief.

Output exactly one line, nothing else. No prose, no fences, no explanation
before or after.

SOUND
UNSOUND: <the one unit that fails, and what in the brief contradicts it or fails to support it — or the one thing the brief asks for that no unit builds>

The brief may carry a "Clarifications" or "Referenced context" block. Those are
the user's own words, answers they gave and turns they wrote, and they are part
of the brief. A unit that builds one of them is SOUND.

The split is SOUND when a careful reader of the brief alone would accept it:
every unit traces to something the brief asks for, and everything the brief asks
for is covered by some unit.

The split is UNSOUND in exactly two cases:

- A unit builds work, structure, or behavior the brief never asked for, or
  contradicts something the brief says.
- Something the brief plainly asks for appears in no unit at all.

Name one. Report the most consequential; do not list several.

You are not judging how the work was divided. The number of units, where the
lines fall, what order they run in, and how big each one is are all engineering
choices you cannot evaluate without seeing the repository, and they are not what
this check is for. Setup, wiring, migration, and test-support units are ordinary
parts of building the thing and are SOUND even when the brief never names them.
"The brief does not mention this unit" is not a reason to reject.

Worked examples, for the brief "add an admin page where I can see uploads and
delete the failed ones":

- Units "add an uploads list endpoint", "add the admin page", "add delete for
  failed uploads". SOUND. Each traces to the brief and together they cover it.
- The same three plus "add a database migration for an uploads index". SOUND.
  Scaffolding the work needs, not new behavior.
- The same three plus "add role-based permissions so only admins can open the
  page". UNSOUND. The brief asks for an admin page, not an authorization system.
- Only "add an uploads list endpoint" and "add the admin page". UNSOUND.
  Deleting failed uploads is asked for and no unit builds it.
- One unit, "build the admin page with listing and delete". SOUND. One large unit
  is a division choice, not a scope error.

You do not see the repository, the plan, or the code. Do not judge feasibility,
sequencing, style, effort, or whether the work is worth doing. Do not propose a
better split, extra units, or an implementation.

Say SOUND when the split holds. Rejecting costs a revision and a second
rejection stops the campaign, so reject only what would build the wrong thing.
